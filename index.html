A little about me

Aloha! 🌊🌺 I’m Chloe, a software engineer with 4 years of experience. Raised in Hawaiʻi with a passion for problem-solving. Driven by curiosity, creativity, and kuleana.

I’m a software engineer with experience building and supporting production systems, with a strong focus on backend development, database-driven applications, and system reliability. In my current role, I develop and maintain production database systems and backend data workflows that support 26 city departments and over one million users.

My work includes SQL Server migrations across legacy and modern environments, production troubleshooting, automation of database processes, and secure access management across large-scale systems.

Previously, I was part of Capgemini’s Next Gen Talent (NGT) Program, a leadership development program for high-potential engineers, where I progressed from Software Engineer to Senior Software Engineer.

I hold a B.S. in Computer Science and a B.A. in Political Science, graduating cum laude from Hawai‘i Pacific University, where I served as Student Body President during my senior year.

Outside of software, I’m interested in the intersection of public policy and technology, particularly algorithmic decision-making in government and the ethical implications of data and AI in public life.
